About

I am a Ugandan technology, finance and business-development professional with a background in information technology, computer engineering, data science, financial analysis and entrepreneurship.

That mix lets me work through a whole project: define the business problem, design the solution, build and deploy it, then analyze the data and improve how the organization operates.

I am Business Development Manager at New Generation Healing and Empowerment Centre (NEGHEC) in Nebbi, and I build software and data systems alongside that role. My earlier ICT and project experience includes work with MTN Uganda, Huawei Technologies, ADVISEM and Albinism Umbrella.
