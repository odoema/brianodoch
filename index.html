Software. Data. AI. Business. Africa.

Brian Odoch Emmanuel is a Ugandan technology and business professional working at the intersection of software development, data science, digital transformation and business systems.

His technical interests and work span web and mobile applications, data collection and analysis, database-backed systems, financial technology and technology-enabled entrepreneurship. He works with tools including Python, JavaScript, Flutter, SQL, Supabase, GitHub and modern web technologies.

His background combines a Bachelor of Commerce with a finance major, a degree in Information Systems and a Diploma in Computer Engineering. He is also pursuing advanced study in Financial Engineering, extending his quantitative and analytical toolkit.

With experience in digital data-collection systems including Open Data Kit (ODK), and in business development, Brian approaches problems from both the system-builder's and the organisation's point of view.
